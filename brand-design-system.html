Section 01

Brand Core

Who The Muscle Engineer is, what we stand for, and the story every piece of work must serve. Read this before touching a pixel or writing a caption.

Positioning

The Muscle Engineer is science-backed, accountability-driven online fitness coaching from India. Founded in 2024 by Coach Amar — a certified fitness expert, professional model and Mr. Supranational South India 2023, with over 10 years in fitness — we engineer transformations: fully personalised training, nutrition and lifestyle systems, delivered 1-on-1, protected by a 100% money back guarantee. We are not an app, not a template, not a guru. We are engineers of the human result.

The metrics rule: "10+ Years" always refers to Coach Amar's experience in fitness — never the company's age. The company line is "Est. 2024". Never blur the two.

"Engineer the strongest, healthiest version of every client — through science, accountability and one hundred honest days at a time."

Vision

Section 02

Logo System

The icon is the soul of the brand and it is permanently locked. The wordmark and tagline around it have been modernised to the digital identity. This section is law.

Rule zero — the icon is sacred. A happy person stands at the centre, arms raised; two arrows beside the figure indicate the trimming and shaping of the body. The mark reads as the letter M, and viewed counter-clockwise, as the letter E. This story is why the icon is never redrawn, retraced, reshaped or "refreshed" — by anyone, ever, including this document. Every appearance of the icon anywhere must come from the official master files in Section 15.

Icon (official file, untouched) + wordmark set live in Bebas Neue + tagline in Space Mono. Because the text is live type beside the real icon file, any team can rebuild this lockup perfectly in HTML, decks or social tools without requesting new artwork. "Engineer" always takes Engine Red.

Section 04

Typography

Three typefaces, three jobs, zero exceptions. Bebas Neue shouts, Inter explains, Space Mono engineers. All free via Google Fonts.

Aa Bebas Neue Display · Headlines · Always Uppercase

IGNITE. IMPROVE. INSPIRE. ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ 0123456789

Weight 400 only — never bold, never italic. Letter-spacing .02 to .04em. Line-height .96 to 1.05.

Aa Inter Body · UI · 300 to 700

Science and passion meet to shape your fitness journey. abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789

Body runs Light 300 at line-height 1.8. Emphasis at 600 to 700 in white. Sentence case always.

Aa Space Mono Labels · Data · Technical

100 DAYS CHALLENGE · MONEY BACK GUARANTEE · 130+ TRANSFORMATIONS

Section labels, tags, stats, buttons, fine print. Uppercase, 700, letter-spacing .12 to .24em.

Cambria is retired. The original lockup's Cambria wordmark belongs to the legacy print era only. Cambria — and every other typeface (Roboto, Montserrat, Poppins, Lato, Oswald, Anton) — is banned from new work in any medium. Three fonts. No exceptions. System fonts exist only as code fallbacks.

Section 07

Photography & Art Direction

Real people, real grain, real effort. Photography is proof — treat it like evidence, not decoration.

Approved

Real clients and real training. Gym grit, sweat, honest lighting. Before/after pairs shot same pose, same distance, same light. Coach Amar candid and working. Grayscale-base treatment with colour on interaction. Dark, moody backgrounds that let red signage breathe.

Rejected

Stock models with studio smiles. Neon gym aesthetics. AI-generated physiques or "enhanced" results — instant brand death for a guarantee-led business. Watermarked images. Oversaturated colour. Anything that looks like a supplement ad.

Signature Treatments — Live

Before / After

Grayscale at rest, colour + 1.05 scale on hover

Scanline overlay — hero portraits only

Clipped frame + red border — features

The consent law. No client photo, result or testimonial goes public — website, social, decks, anywhere — without explicit written consent on record. This is promised in our Terms and it is absolute. Transformation crops stay 3:4, faces may be cropped or blurred on request, and the "Before / After" tag is always honest: same person, real timeline, no editing of bodies.
Section 08

Motion Standards

Fast, purposeful, engineered. One easing curve runs the whole brand. If an animation doesn't clarify hierarchy or reward intent, it doesn't exist.

The one easing: cubic-bezier(.22, .86, .36, 1) — var(--ease). Confident start, soft landing. No bounce, no elastic, no spring. Ever.
Scroll Revealopacity 0 to 1 · translateY 28px to 0 · .8s var(--ease)

IntersectionObserver at 10% threshold. Stagger siblings with .1s delay steps (delay-1 through delay-8).

Stat Counter1800ms · cubic ease-out · fires at 40% visible

Numbers count up once, suffix in red. Demo: 0+

Card LifttranslateY(-4 to -6px) · border turns red · .3s

Hover on cards. Shadow deepens with lift; never scale a card.

Button Ripple + Shimmerripple 600ms · shimmer sweep on hover

Red CTAs only. Ripple expands from click point at 35% white.

Marquee Ticker28s linear infinite · pauses on hover

Duplicated track, aria-hidden on the clone. Red band, black diamonds.

Hero Parallaxfactor 0.08 · disabled on touch

Photo drifts against scroll. Subtle — if you notice it moving, it's too much.

Reduced motion is sacred. When prefers-reduced-motion is set, everything above dies instantly: reveals render visible, counters print final values, marquee freezes, parallax and custom cursor disable. This is shipped behaviour — never remove it.

Mechanics

Always

Sentence case in body copy. Uppercase lives only in Bebas display and mono labels. Short sentences, then explanation. Active voice. Specific numbers over adjectives. The allowed punctuation set beyond ASCII: · — – ' ' " " … and nothing else.

Never

Emojis — anywhere, including captions and DMs. Exclamation marks in marketing copy. ALL-CAPS body sentences. More than one CTA per message. A guarantee mention without its asterisk where it stands as a claim (see the asterisk discipline in Section 14).

Section 12

Web Standards

For developers. The site is deliberately simple: static, framework-free, fast. Keep it that way.

Stack & Architecture

Layer Decision Why
Hosting GitHub Pages, static HTML/CSS/JS Free, fast, no server to maintain.
Pages Self-contained: CSS in <style>, JS at the bottom of each page Zero build step, zero broken imports; owner can edit one file in Notepad.
JavaScript ES5-style IIFE, no frameworks, no dependencies Loads instantly, never breaks from a CDN outage.
Blog engine blogs/data.json is the single source of truth One JSON entry updates homepage teaser, blog grid, filters and related posts.
Forms fetch POST to Google Apps Script, saves to Sheets + emails the founder Honeypot field "company" silently absorbs bots. SCRIPT_URL lives in index.html.
Analytics GA4 + Meta Pixel, routed through Google Tag Manager when deployed Cookie policy on terms.html already covers them. Never hardcode pixels outside GTM.

Page Map

Non-Negotiable Patterns

Image Fallback Chain

Every Wix-hosted image carries onerror handling; the logo falls back local file to CDN to hidden. Image boxes show the branded placeholder when a source dies — the site never shows a broken-image icon.

One H1 Per Page

Exactly one h1, OG tags on every page, descriptive alt text everywhere, noindex on legal and internal docs. Headings nest in order — no skipping levels for styling reasons.

Accessibility Floor

aria-expanded on accordions and the burger, focus-visible never removed, contrast per Section 03, prefers-reduced-motion fully honoured, touch targets 44px minimum.

Token Discipline

No raw hex in component CSS — only var(--token). New colours don't get invented; if a value isn't in Section 13, it doesn't exist. Run a hex-grep before every ship.

Section 14

Do's & Don'ts

The non-negotiables, paired. Every collaborator — developer, designer, intern, agency — signs off on these before shipping anything.

Use official logo files only

Every icon placement comes from the master exports in Section 15, in red, white or black, on an approved background, with 1x clear space.

Never redraw the icon

No tracing, no AI regeneration, no "cleaner version", no simplification. The M, the E, the person, the arrows — locked forever.

One red: #E8221A

Engine Red in every new asset, kept near 10% of any composition. Hover state is #C01A14 and nothing else.

No green, no new hues

No green success states, no blue links, no gradients between hues, no Legacy Red #aa1e22 in new work. The palette is closed.

Three fonts, three jobs

Bebas Neue displays, Inter explains, Space Mono labels. Loaded from Google Fonts with display=swap.

No Cambria, no new fonts

Cambria retired with the legacy lockup. No Canva default fonts sneaking into social posts. No bold or italic Bebas — it has neither.

Asterisk discipline

Every prominent "money back guarantee" claim carries a superscript asterisk, a nearby "T&C apply" footnote, and the terms.html link. Web, social, decks — everywhere.

No unconditional promises

Never "guaranteed results", never weight-loss numbers with deadlines, never cure language — especially around PCOS. Claims we can't evidence don't ship.

Consent before publishing

Written client consent on file before any photo, result or testimonial appears anywhere. Same pose, same light, honest timeline on every before/after.

No emojis, no fake physiques

Zero emojis on any surface including captions. Zero AI-generated or retouched bodies — a guarantee brand survives on believability.

Sharp, dark, textured

Zero border-radius, black-family backgrounds, exactly one whisper-opacity texture per surface, the 13px corner clip on red CTAs.

No light-mode drift

No white-background digital layouts, no rounded "friendly" cards, no soft pastel rebrands for festive posts. Festivals get our black and red too.
