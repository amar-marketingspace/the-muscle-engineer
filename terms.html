Money Back Guarantee & Refund Policy

We put a 100% money back guarantee on our programs because we are confident in our system — and because we believe risk should sit with the coach, not the client. The guarantee is real, and like every honest guarantee it has conditions. They exist for one reason: the guarantee can only apply when the program was actually followed, so that results (or their absence) can be attributed to our coaching with evidence.

What The Guarantee Is

If you remain fully accountable with us throughout your program — following everything exactly as instructed — and you still do not achieve the results agreed at your assessment, we will review your case as described below, and if our review establishes with scientific evidence that our programming failed despite your compliance, you receive a 100% refund of your program fee.

Eligibility Conditions

All of the following conditions must be satisfied for a refund claim under the guarantee:

  1. Full accountability throughout the journey. You followed the plan and protocols exactly as instructed, for the full duration of the program.
  2. Surprise check-ins. During the program you will receive unannounced check-ins — these may include a request for a same-day photo, video or call — and you must cooperate with them. They keep the guarantee honest for everyone.
  3. Daily records. You submitted your workout videos every training day (Sundays excluded) and photos of every meal as advised, through the Google Form we provide.
  4. The 80% threshold. Across the full program, you submitted at least 80% of the required records (workout videos and meal photos as advised).
  5. Honest disclosure. You disclosed all allergies, injuries, medical conditions and relevant habits at the time of assessment, without hiding anything.
  6. Adverse effects reported. If anything felt off or caused negative effects during the program, you stopped the activity, informed your coach immediately, and consulted a doctor as advised.

The Review Process

If the conditions above are met and the agreed results were not achieved, we will arrange relevant tests and an expert review of your case from our side. If that review establishes, with scientific evidence, that the failure lies with our programming rather than with adherence, health factors that were withheld, or circumstances outside coaching control — we will refund you, for sure.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 14 business days of approval, to the original payment method wherever possible, or to a bank account you designate.

Everything Else Is Non-Refundable

Except as provided under the Money Back Guarantee above, fees once paid are not refundable in any case — including change of mind, personal scheduling conflicts, partial completion, non-participation, missed check-ins or failure to submit records. The pause plan feature exists precisely so that life events do not cost you your program. Nothing in this policy limits rights you hold under applicable Indian consumer protection law that cannot be waived by agreement.

In one line: do the work, document the work, be honest with us — and if we still fail you, every rupee comes back.

We take your privacy seriously — coaching only works on trust. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information The Muscle Engineer collects when you visit this website, submit an enquiry or enroll in a program, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. Because fitness coaching involves health-related information, we treat your data with particular care.

What Information We Collect

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They let the website — or third-party services it uses — remember information about your visit, such as pages viewed or whether you have visited before. They are widely used and essential to how most modern websites and advertising work.

Tools We Use Or May Deploy

The following tools cover our current and planned analytics and advertising setup. This policy applies to each tool from the moment it is active on the website; we use cookies for no purpose beyond what is described here.

Tool Provider Purpose Type
Google Analytics (GA4) Google LLC Measures website traffic, page views, session duration, referral sources and user behaviour, to help us understand how visitors use the website. Analytics
Google Tag Manager Google LLC Manages and deploys tracking scripts (including Google Analytics and other tags) without modifying website code directly. GTM itself does not set analytics cookies, but enables the tools that do. Functional
Meta Pixel (Facebook / Instagram) Meta Platforms Inc. Tracks website visits to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and enables retargeting — showing our ads to people who previously visited this website. Marketing

Cookie Categories

  • Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the website. Data is aggregated and anonymised where possible, and used to improve performance and content.
  • Marketing cookies track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements. Data may be shared with advertising platforms (Meta) for campaign optimisation and retargeting.
  • Functional cookies enable tag-management infrastructure (Google Tag Manager) to operate correctly.

Data Processed By Third Parties

These tools are operated by third parties, and using our website may subject you to their data collection as well. We encourage you to review their policies: Google Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.
